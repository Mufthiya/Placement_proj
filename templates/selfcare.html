SEGMENT 01 · MIND

Handling anxiety & depression

{% if mental_pred and mental_pred not in ['General Mental Health', 'Coping and Resilience'] %}
Your last assessment noted: {{ mental_pred }}. The steps below are a starting point, not a replacement for professional care.
{% endif %}

When anxiety spikes

  • 4-7-8 breathing: inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s. Repeat four rounds.
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding: name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
  • Set a worry window: 15 scheduled minutes a day to worry on paper; outside it, note and postpone.
  • Cut stimulants after 2pm: caffeine and nicotine mimic and amplify anxiety.

When depression pulls you down

  • Shrink the task: not "clean the house", just "put one dish away". Momentum beats motivation.
  • Sunlight + movement: a 10-minute morning walk is clinically meaningful.
  • One human contact daily: message or call someone, even briefly.
  • Protect sleep: fixed wake time matters more than bed time.
  • If heaviness lasts over two weeks or you have thoughts of self-harm, contact a professional or a helpline today - Life's Appointment page can route you fast.

SEGMENT 02 · BODY

Maintaining physical health

{% if phys_pred == 'Positive' %}
Your latest physical check-in flagged a possible issue: keep your physician in the loop while following these habits.
{% endif %}

Daily basics

  • Move: aim for 150 minutes of moderate activity weekly - brisk walking counts.
  • Sleep: 7-9 hours; same wake-up time even on weekends.
  • Hydrate & eat colour: water first; half the plate vegetables and fruit.
  • Screen breaks: every 45 minutes, stand and look 6 metres away for 20 seconds.

Watch & prevent

  • Blood pressure: check monthly if it has ever read High.
  • No smoking, less alcohol: single biggest wins for heart and lungs.
  • Regular screenings: annual BP, sugar and cholesterol after age 30.
  • Log symptoms early: run a Life check-in when something feels off - early patterns are easier to treat.
